Automatic profiles

Only apps you add receive an app profile. Unknown foreground state releases profile limits. Battery saver, low battery and heat take priority.

Refresh overrides temporarily change Android display preferences while the app is foreground. Only advertised modes are accepted. A ROM setting change pauses control. Use “Let the ROM decide” if you already configured that app in your ROM. Android display preferences persist across reboots: release controls before immediately disabling or removing the module. The next enabled boot also attempts cleanup.

Advanced controls

Off by default. These can increase power use and heat. Clock and GPU cooling overrides expire after 60 seconds; charging overrides after 30 minutes at most. Heat, low battery, unplugging or battery saver can end them sooner.
